# LC05: One step is most of a workflow's cost

- **Lens:** Cost and performance
- **Severity:** info
- **From:** cost

When a single step is more than cost.step.share percent (default 60) of the typical cost of its workflow, any saving has to come from that step. What to do: look at its model, prompt length and rounds first.

## Handle it without editing code

`weave review --accept FINGERPRINT --reason "why"` records that the finding is fine. `LC05 = off` in `review.properties` turns the rule off, and `LC05.severity = low` changes how loud it is.
