# LC06: A retried step can be expensive in the worst case

- **Lens:** Cost and performance
- **Severity:** low
- **From:** cost

Each retry pays for the whole call again. When the worst case of a retried step is above cost.retry.limit (default 1.00, in the currency of your price table) the finding is raised. It needs a price table (--prices). What to do: retry fewer times or make the step cheaper.

## Handle it without editing code

`weave review --accept FINGERPRINT --reason "why"` records that the finding is fine. `LC06 = off` in `review.properties` turns the rule off, and `LC06.severity = low` changes how loud it is.
