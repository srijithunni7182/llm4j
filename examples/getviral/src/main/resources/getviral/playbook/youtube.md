# YouTube playbook

## Title
Clear promise plus curiosity, ideally under about 60 characters so it is not truncated. Front-load the keyword people search for. Write 3 options and pick the most specific.

## Thumbnail
One focal subject, high contrast, at most 3–4 words of text that complement (not repeat) the title. Faces with clear emotion and strong colour contrast stand out in a grid.

## Script outline
Hook (first 15 seconds restates the title promise), context, main sections with pattern changes every minute or so, payoff, end screen pointing to a related video.

## Description and chapters
First two lines summarise the video with the main keyword. Add timestamped chapters starting at 0:00 so viewers can jump to what they need.

## Shorts cut
Pull the single most surprising moment into a vertical Short (under 60 seconds) that teases the long video.
