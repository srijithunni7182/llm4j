# Trust and safety

## No invented facts
Never invent statistics, studies, quotes or results. If a number is needed, verify it with a source or phrase it as personal experience.

## Health, money and legal claims
Avoid guarantees ("lose 10kg in a week", "get rich"). Frame as personal experience and suggest consulting a professional where appropriate.

## Respect and inclusion
Punch up, never down. Roasts and hot takes target ideas and habits, not identities or protected characteristics.

## Attribution
Credit creators of borrowed formats, audio and images; respect image licences (for example Creative Commons attribution requirements).

## Privacy
Never include personal data such as phone numbers, emails or home addresses in public content.
