# Instagram Reels playbook

## First three seconds
Put the hook on screen as text AND say it out loud — many people watch muted. Start with motion or a face; avoid slow intros.

## Beat sheet
Plan the reel in beats of 2–5 seconds: hook, problem, 2–3 value beats, payoff, loop-back line. Each beat names the shot, the voiceover and the on-screen text.

## Length
Short reels (roughly 15–45 seconds) are easier to watch to the end, and a reel that loops cleanly back to its start gets rewatched.

## Caption and hashtags
The caption expands on the reel and ends with a call to action (save, share, comment a keyword). Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags and a 2,200-character caption; 3–8 targeted hashtags usually read better than a wall of tags.

## Audio
Trending audio can help discovery, but choose a track whose mood fits. Keep the voiceover clearly audible over music.

## Cover
Design a cover frame with 3–5 words of large text so the reel looks good on the profile grid.
