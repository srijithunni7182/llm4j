# Retention tactics

## Open loops
Promise something early ("the third tip is the one that changed everything") and deliver it later to keep people watching or reading.

## Specificity beats hype
Concrete numbers, names and steps feel credible. "Walk 10 minutes after lunch" beats "move more".

## Visual rhythm
Change the shot, angle, text or B-roll every few seconds in short-form video. Static talking heads lose attention fast.

## Payoff before the call to action
Give the value first, then ask for the follow or save. Asking too early feels transactional.

## Loopable endings
End short videos on a line that flows back into the first frame, which encourages rewatches.
